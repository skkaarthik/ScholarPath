GREAT Scholarships at Trinity Laban

Trinity Laban is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • China
  • India

The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.
