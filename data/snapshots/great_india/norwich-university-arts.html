GREAT Scholarships at Norwich University of the Arts

Norwich University of the Arts is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • China
  • India

The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at Norwich University of the Arts

To find out more about GREAT Scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit Norwich University of the Arts's website. The deadline for applications is 3 May 2026.
