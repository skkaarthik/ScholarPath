GREAT Scholarships at the Royal College of Art

The Royal College of Art is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • India
  • Turkey

The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

Apply for a GREAT Scholarships at the Royal College of Art

To find out more about GREAT scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit the Royal College of Art's website. The deadline for applications is 24 April 2026.
