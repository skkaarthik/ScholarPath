GREAT Scholarships at the University of Kent

The University of Kent is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • Ghana
  • India
  • Kenya
  • Malaysia
  • Nigeria
  • Thailand
  • Turkey

The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at the University of Kent

To find out about the GREAT Scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit the University of Kent's website. The deadline for applications is 24 April 2026, 5pm BST.
