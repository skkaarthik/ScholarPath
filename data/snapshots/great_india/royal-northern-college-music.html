GREAT Scholarships at the Royal Northern College of Music

Royal Northern College of Music is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • India
  • Thailand

The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at Royal Northern College of Music

To find out more about GREAT Scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit RNCM's website. The deadline for applications is 1 August 2026.
