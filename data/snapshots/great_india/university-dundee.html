GREAT Scholarships at University of Dundee

University of Dundee is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • India
  • Ghana
  • Kenya

The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at University of Dundee

To find out more about GREAT Scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit University of Dundee's website. The deadline for applications is 15 May 2026.
