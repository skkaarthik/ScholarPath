GREAT Scholarships at University of Surrey

University of Surrey is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • India
  • Kenya
  • Spain

The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at University of Surrey

To find out more about GREAT Scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit the University of Surrey's website. The deadline for applications is 29 May 2026.
