GREAT Scholarships at the University of Bristol

The University of Bristol is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £13,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • India
  • Mexico
  • Thailand

The scholarships are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at the University of Bristol

To find out more about the GREAT Scholarships on offer and to apply, visit the University of Bristol's website. The deadline for applications is 10 April 2026.
