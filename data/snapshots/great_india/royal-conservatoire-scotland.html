GREAT Scholarships at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland

The Royal Conservatoire of Scotland is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • China
  • India

These scholarships are available for a taught master’s programme in the following subjects:

  • MA Collaborative Piano
  • MA Chamber Music
  • MA Composition
  • MA Conducting
  • MA Jazz
  • MA Joint Principal Study
  • MA Performance
  • MA Performance and Musicology
  • MA Performance and Pedagogy
  • MA Piano for Dance
  • MA Repetiteurship
  • MA Traditional Music
  • MA/MFA Acting Classical and Contemporary Text
  • MA/MFA Directing Classical and Contemporary Text
  • MA Musical Theatre (Directing)
  • MA/MFA Production (Design)
  • MA/MFA Production (Management)

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland

To find out more about GREAT scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland's website. The deadline for applications is 31 May 2026.
