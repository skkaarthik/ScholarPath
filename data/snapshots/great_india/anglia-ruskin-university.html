GREAT Scholarships at Anglia Ruskin University

Anglia Ruskin University is offering GREAT Scholarships for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • Ghana
  • India
  • Indonesia

The scholarships for Ghana are available for a taught master’s programme in the following subjects:

  • MA Education
  • MBA FinTech and Data Analytics
  • MSc Healthcare Leadership and Management
  • MSc Public Health and Community Wellbeing
  • MSc Accounting and Finance

The scholarships for India are available for a taught master’s programme in the following subjects:

  • MA Education
  • MBA FinTech and Data Analytics
  • MSc Healthcare Leadership and Management
  • MSc Automation and Robotics
  • MSc Crop Production
  • MSc Pharmaceutical Science

The scholarships for Indonesia are available for all full-time postgraduate taught master's programmes.

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at Anglia Ruskin University

To find out more about GREAT Scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit the Anglia Ruskin University's website. The deadline for applications is 30 April 2026.
