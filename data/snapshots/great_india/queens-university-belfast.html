GREAT Scholarships at Queen's University Belfast

Queen's University Belfast is offering GREAT Scholarship for the 2026-27 academic year, each worth £10,000.

Scholarships are available for students from:

  • India
  • Malaysia

These scholarships are available for a taught master's programme in the following subjects related to justice and law:

  • LLM Criminology and Criminal Justice
  • LLM International Commercial and Business Law
  • LLM International Human Rights Law
  • LLM Intellectual Property Law
  • LLM Law
  • LLM Law and Technology

Apply for a GREAT Scholarship at Queen's University Belfast

To find out more about GREAT scholarships on offer and how to apply, visit the Queen's University Belfast's website. The deadline for applications is 31 May 2026.
